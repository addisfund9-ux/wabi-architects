How We Work — 02

Five steps,
no hand-offs

We don't pass your project to another firm halfway. The team that draws it is the team that finishes it.

  1. 01Concept

    We study the site, the brief and how you'll use the space.

  2. 02Design

    Architecture, interiors and 3D visuals you can see before anything is built.

  3. 03Detailing

    Working drawings, materials and finishes, chosen and specified by us.

  4. 04Finishing

    Our own crews lay the tile, marble and gypsum exactly as drawn.

  5. 05Handover

    We supervise to the last tile and hand you a finished space.

One WABI team · one contract · one point of responsibility
