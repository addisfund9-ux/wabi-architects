Studio — 01

Remarkable spaces, made with careful attention to detail — where Ethiopian heritage meets modern form.

Established by innovative architects, WABI provides consulting, architecture, engineering, interior design, finishing and construction management for residential, commercial and institutional clients.

We design spaces that captivate visually and sit naturally in their surroundings, drawing on the rich cultural heritage of Ethiopia.

Then our own finishing crews carry those drawings onto site — tile, marble and gypsum laid exactly as specified, so nothing gets lost between the plan and the floor.
