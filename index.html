Design & Finishing — 05

One studio,
from drawing to tile

We don't hand your project over halfway. The team that draws it is the team that finishes it, so nothing gets lost between the plan and the floor.

01Concept

  1. We study the site, the brief and how you'll use the space.

  2. Architecture, interiors and 3D visuals you can see before anything is built.

  3. Working drawings, materials and finishes, chosen and specified by us.

  4. Our own crews lay the tile, marble and gypsum exactly as drawn.

  5. We supervise to the last tile and hand you a finished space.

One WABI team · one contract · one point of responsibility
